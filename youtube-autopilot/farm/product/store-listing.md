## Short description (one line)

## Description
How much does it really cost to keep a beef cow? Is your herd making money, or only covering cash costs?

This spreadsheet answers that in about two minutes, using the same USDA numbers we use on **The Farm Ledger** YouTube channel.

**What's inside**
- **Herd Calculator:** your income, cash costs, all-in costs, profit per cow, feed cost per day, and the **breakeven calf price** you need to cover costs.
- **Feed & Hay Estimator:** tons of hay for your winter and what it will cost.
- **Beef From a Steer:** hanging weight, retail beef and what that beef is worth at the grocery store.
- **Price Reference:** every default number with its USDA source and date. Swap in your own local prices anytime.

**Built for:** small cow-calf operations, homesteaders thinking about cattle, 4-H and FFA families, and anyone curious about ranch money.

Pre-filled with 2025–2026 USDA national averages (ERS Commodity Costs and Returns, NASS/AMS livestock prices, ERS Meat Price Spreads). Works in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

*Estimates only, not financial advice. Your costs and prices will differ.*
