# GTA 6 fact sheet

As of 2026-11-08, summarised from Wikipedia's "Grand Theft Auto VI" article. Shorts may use ONLY these facts.
**[confirmed]** means Rockstar or Take-Two said it; **[reported]** means press or industry sources; **[estimate]** means an analyst figure. Refresh this sheet after launch.

## Release
- [confirmed] Release date: 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
- [confirmed] No PC version at launch. [reported] Industry sources say PC is planned for Q1 2027.
- [confirmed] Price: $79.99 (standard edition).
- [confirmed] Physical copies hit shops on 12 November and contain a download code, not a disc, so you can pre-load a week early. Some players criticised the lack of discs, and some retailers refused to stock it.
- [confirmed] It runs at 30 FPS on both consoles at launch (GTA 5 ran at 60 FPS on newer consoles).
- [confirmed] No microtransactions in either edition at launch, and no generative AI in the game.
- [confirmed] Ultimate Edition adds clothing, hairstyles, locations, missions, tattoos, vehicles and weapons. Pre-orders get the Vintage Vice City Pack, inspired by GTA: Vice City (2002) and Tommy Vercetti.
- [confirmed] Delayed twice: first from 2025 to 26 May 2026 (announced May 2025), then to 19 November 2026 for extra polish (announced November 2025).
- [estimate] About 6.45 million pre-orders and $630 million in pre-order revenue by October 2026; 74% of pre-orders on PS5.
- [estimate] Newzoo estimated $260 million in pre-orders in the first 7 days, and predicted 37–51 million copies sold in week one.
- [estimate] Niko Partners: over $1.2 billion in first-day sales. DFC Intelligence: $3.2 billion in the first year.

## World
- [confirmed] Set in Leonida, a fictional US state based on Florida.
- [confirmed] Vice City is based on Miami. Grassrivers is based on the Everglades, and the Leonida Keys on the Florida Keys.
- [confirmed] Other locations: Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga National Park and Port Gellhorn.
- [reported] The map is about three times bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2's playable area.
- [confirmed] The world parodies 2020s America: social media and influencers, police body cameras, and memes like "Florida Man".

## Characters and story
- [confirmed] Two protagonists: Jason Duval, an Army veteran who worked for drug runners in the Leonida Keys, and Lucia Caminos, who was in Leonida Penitentiary after fighting for her family from Liberty City.
- [confirmed] Lucia is the series' first non-optional female protagonist.
- [confirmed] Their dynamic is inspired by Bonnie and Clyde. After a heist goes wrong, they get caught up in a state-wide conspiracy.
- [confirmed] Supporting characters: Cal Hampton (Jason's paranoid friend), Boobie Ike (runs a Vice City business empire), Dre'Quan Priest (record label Only Raw Records), Real Dimez (the duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista (veteran bank robber), and Brian Heder (Jason's landlord, a long-time drug runner).

## Gameplay
- [confirmed] Single-player, third-person, played as Jason or Lucia. You can split them up or have them hang out together.
- [confirmed] Activities include skydiving, wrestling, basketball, working out, kayaking and scuba diving.
- [confirmed] Characters can gain or lose weight, and staying awake for days shows on their faces.
- [confirmed] A "Criminal Profile" tracks whether each character keeps crimes professional or commits needless violence, with consequences.
- [confirmed] Snapmatic, an in-game social media app, parodies TikTok and Instagram.
- [confirmed] Hair, clothes and accessories are customisable.

## Records and hype
- [confirmed] Trailer 1 (December 2023) leaked a day early. It broke YouTube records and passed GTA 5's reveal-trailer lifetime views within 2 days (101 million). Its song was Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road".
- [confirmed] Trailer 2 (6 May 2025) got a record 475 million views across platforms in 24 hours, and won Best Game Trailer at the 2025 Golden Joysticks. (A Spider-Man trailer beat that record in March 2026.)
- [confirmed] A pre-launch trailer released 15 October 2026.
- [confirmed] The 2022 leak of unfinished footage is called one of the biggest leaks in video game history.
- [confirmed] "Before GTA 6" became a meme about surprising events happening before the game's release.
- [confirmed] Other publishers moved their release dates to avoid competing with it.
- [confirmed] Rockstar's development teams more than doubled in size since Red Dead Redemption 2. Production started in 2020.

## Launch marketing
- [confirmed] Since 1 October, the Kaseya Center (Miami Heat arena) roof has read "Welcome to Vice City"; the sign cost $975,000.
- [confirmed] Wrapped cars with Leonida plates, a Vice City blimp and Vice City beach umbrellas have appeared in Miami.
- [confirmed] On 18 November, the day before launch, the Miami Heat play as "Vice City" against the Milwaukee Bucks.
- [confirmed] Netflix premiered an "extended look" on 27 August 2026; Netflix and Twitch had technical issues from the demand.
- [confirmed] GTA VI: The Album has 34 original songs. There are also themed DualSense controllers.
