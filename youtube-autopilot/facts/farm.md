### Eggs (USDA ERS Food Price Outlook, Summary Findings, updated 11/24/2026)
Source: https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings (fetched Nov 25, 2026; ERS is reachable from this environment)
- Retail egg prices: −0.1% Sept→Oct 2026. Forecast for 2026: **−29.5%** (interval −30.8 to −28.0).
- Retail egg prices rose 32.2% (2022), 1.4% (2023), 8.5% (2024), **21.9% (2025)**.
- Cause, as ERS states it: the HPAI (bird flu) outbreak that began in 2022 raised egg prices by reducing egg-layer flocks and egg production. USDA projects higher egg production in 2026 than 2025, and replacement pullets have been sufficient in 2026.
- Farm-level egg prices: −7.8% Sept→Oct 2026; **−74.4% Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025**; forecast **−81.9% in 2026** (interval −83.0 to −79.8). Farm-level eggs rose 163.1% (2022), fell 28.3% (2023), rose 43.1% (2024) and 31.6% (2025).
- Don't use the line "20.6 percent lower in October 2026 than in September 2025" (the months don't match, so it's probably a typo on the page).
