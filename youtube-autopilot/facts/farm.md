## Sourced numbers (add as found)

### Farm income (USDA ERS, Highlights from the Farm Income Forecast, updated 9/3/2026)
Source: https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-economy/farm-sector-income-finances/highlights-from-the-farm-income-forecast (fetched Nov 27, 2026; check for the early-December update before reusing)
- Net farm income 2026 forecast: **$158.4 billion**, −$4.3B (−2.6%) vs 2025 nominal; −5.5% inflation-adjusted; still above the 2006–25 average (inflation-adjusted).
- Net cash farm income 2026: $176.4B, +0.4% nominal (−2.5% inflation-adjusted).
- **Average net cash farm income for farm businesses: $121,700 per farm in 2026, +7.1% vs 2025** (nominal). Farm businesses = gross cash farm income ≥ $350,000, or < $350,000 with farming as the operator's primary occupation.
- 6 of 9 ERS Farm Resource Regions are expected to see average net cash farm income rise; the Northern Great Plains has the largest increase.
- Crop-specialized farm businesses: higher average net cash farm income in 2026, except specialty crops. **All animal/animal-product specializations: lower average net farm income** (as printed).
- Farm sector equity: forecast +$102.9B (nominal) in 2026.

### Dairy / milk (USDA ERS Food Price Outlook, Summary Findings, updated 11/24/2026)
Source: https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings
- Farm-level milk: +10.8% Sept→Oct 2026, but −0.4% Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025; forecast **−5.3% in 2026** (interval −7.4 to −2.7).
- Retail dairy: +0.6% Sept→Oct 2026; forecast −0.1% in 2026 (interval −0.4 to +0.1).
- ERS: USDA projects milk production up in 2026 over 2025, driven by a larger dairy herd and more milk per cow; higher milk output is expected to put downward pressure on cheese and butter prices despite strong domestic and export demand.
- posted: CYpw57lHTWc, #32, live Nov 26 6 PM CT ("Are Milk Prices Going Down?")

### Poultry (same source): retail +0.4% Sept→Oct 2026; forecast +1.2% in 2026.

### Eggs (USDA ERS Food Price Outlook, Summary Findings, updated 11/24/2026)
Source: https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/food-price-outlook/summary-findings (fetched Nov 25, 2026; ERS is reachable from this environment)
- Retail egg prices: −0.1% Sept→Oct 2026. Forecast for 2026: **−29.5%** (interval −30.8 to −28.0).
- Retail egg prices rose 32.2% (2022), 1.4% (2023), 8.5% (2024), **21.9% (2025)**.
- Cause, as ERS states it: the HPAI (bird flu) outbreak that began in 2022 raised egg prices by reducing egg-layer flocks and egg production. USDA projects higher egg production in 2026 than 2025, and replacement pullets have been sufficient in 2026.
- Farm-level egg prices: −7.8% Sept→Oct 2026; **−74.4% Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025**; forecast **−81.9% in 2026** (interval −83.0 to −79.8). Farm-level eggs rose 163.1% (2022), fell 28.3% (2023), rose 43.1% (2024) and 31.6% (2025).
- Don't use the line "20.6 percent lower in October 2026 than in September 2025" (the months don't match, so it's probably a typo on the page).
