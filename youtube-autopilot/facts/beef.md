# Beef & cattle fact sheet

Every number in a beef Short must come from this sheet or from a source you add to it with a link.
Refresh the USDA numbers each month: https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/meat-price-spreads
(CSV: "Choice beef values and spreads and the all-fresh retail value").

| 5-market fed steer price ($/cwt live) | 221.05 | 226.95 | 222.56 |
| All-fresh beef retail value (¢/lb) | 946.8 | 966.3 | 884.2 |

Notes:
- "Farmers' share" covers everyone who raised the animal: the cow-calf rancher, any stocker, and the feedlot.
- The packer share swings month to month. Say "about" and name the month.
