# The Cattle Ledger — Short #13: "Why a Calf Is Worth More Per Pound Than a Steer 🤔"

**Length:** 26 s · **Voice:** vidIQ "Brian" · Stock footage only (no AI shot; balance under 800)
**Live:** https://youtube.com/shorts/VNRQe2PwiCg

## Script
> A five-hundred-pound calf brings almost eighty percent more per pound than a finished steer. In September, USDA had those calves at about three ninety-four per hundred pounds. Finished steers? About two twenty-two. Buyers pay up for all the growing still ahead. That's roughly nineteen seventy for the calf, and thirty-two hundred for the steer. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (all already in facts/beef.md):
- OKC feeder steers M&L #1, 500–550 lb, Sept 2026: $394.01/cwt (USDA ERS Livestock prices)
- Nebraska direct slaughter steers, Sept 2026: $221.67/cwt (same source)
- 394.01 / 221.67 = 1.78, so the calf brings 78% more per pound
- Per head: 500 lb × $3.94 = $1,970; 1,451 lb (Sept 2026 avg. FI live weight, ERS slaughter weights) × $2.2167 = $3,216
