## Script
> USDA's typical cow-calf ranch cleared about seventy-two thousand dollars over cash costs in 2025. So how much do ranchers actually make? That ranch runs about ninety-eight beef cows. Each cow brought in about fourteen hundred seventeen dollars, mostly from selling calves. Cash costs, like feed, vet bills and fuel, were about six seventy-six per cow. That leaves roughly seven forty per cow. But that seventy-two thousand also has to pay for the rancher's own time and for wearing out equipment. Count those, and the ranch lost about three hundred thirty-five dollars per cow. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (USDA ERS cow-calf 2025, all in facts/beef.md):
- Typical farm: 98 beef cows
- Gross value $1,417.31, of which calves $1,069.53
- Operating costs $676.45
- Value over operating costs $740.86 per cow, so 98 × $740.86 ≈ $72,604
- All-in −$335.13 per cow
