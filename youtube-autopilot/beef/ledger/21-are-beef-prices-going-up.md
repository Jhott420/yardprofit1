## Script
> Beef at the store costs about six percent more than last year, but ranchers are getting less. So are beef prices going up? USDA's Choice beef retail value hit about nine ninety-four a pound in September, up from about nine thirty-eight, the 2025 average. Ground chuck jumped sixteen percent in a year, to about six forty-eight a pound. But the farm side of that pound of Choice beef actually slipped, from about five dollars and two cents to four ninety-one. The ranchers' share of your beef dollar fell from fifty-three percent to forty-nine. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

- Net farm value: 490.7¢ vs 501.6¢
- Farmers' share: 49.3% vs 53.5%

The comparison is a month against the 2025 annual average; the video and description both say so. "Farm" includes feedlots, as the description notes.
