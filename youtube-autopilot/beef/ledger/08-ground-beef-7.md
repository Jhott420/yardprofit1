# The Cattle Ledger — Short #8: "Why Is Ground Beef Almost $7 a Pound? 😳"

**Length:** about 21 s · **Voice:** vidIQ "Brian"

## Script
> Ground beef is six seventy-nine a pound. That's the U.S. average this September, from USDA and Labor Department data. A year earlier, ground chuck was five fifty-eight. Now it's six forty-eight. That's up sixteen percent in one year. And the rancher still gets only about half of every beef dollar. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used:** BLS retail prices via USDA ERS.
- Ground beef, Sept 2026: $6.792
- Ground chuck: $5.576 in Sept 2025, $6.483 in Sept 2026 (+16.3%)
- Farmers' share of the retail beef dollar: 49.3%, Sept 2026 (ERS)
