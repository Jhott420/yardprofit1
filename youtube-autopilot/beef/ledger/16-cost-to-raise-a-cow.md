## Script
> Keeping one beef cow for a year cost about seventeen hundred fifty dollars in 2025, according to USDA. So how much does it cost to raise a cow? Start with feed: about four hundred thirty-six dollars a year, roughly a dollar twenty a day. Add vet bills, fuel and repairs, and the cash costs come to about six seventy-six. Most of the rest is the big part nobody sees: about five seventy for the rancher's own unpaid time, and three eighty for equipment wear. So the cash cost is under seven hundred, but the true cost is more than double that. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (USDA ERS cow-calf, U.S., 2025, $ per cow; all in facts/beef.md): total listed $1,752.44; feed $436.35 ($1.20/day); operating $676.45; unpaid labor $569.78; capital recovery $382.71. 1,752 / 676 = 2.6×, so "more than double".
