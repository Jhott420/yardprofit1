## Script
> A beef cow eats about a dollar twenty of feed every single day. So how much does it cost to feed a cow per day? USDA puts the feed bill at about four hundred thirty-six dollars per cow for 2025. About one eighty-six is hay and grain the ranch grows itself. About one fifty-six is pasture the cow grazes. Only about ninety-five is feed bought off the farm. That makes feed nearly two-thirds of a rancher's cash costs. And alfalfa hay jumped from one seventy-one to two hundred six dollars a ton in a year. Subscribe. Next: how much is ground beef per pound?

**Facts used** (all in facts/beef.md): ERS cow-calf 2025: total feed $436.35 (÷365 ≈ $1.20/day); homegrown harvested $185.58, grazed $155.92, purchased $94.85 (sum 436.35); feed is 64.5% of $676.45 operating costs. Alfalfa hay $171 → $206/ton (Aug 2025 → Aug 2026).
