## Script
> Alfalfa hay is up twenty percent in one year. USDA says it averaged two hundred six dollars a ton this August, up from one seventy-one a year earlier. Grass hay climbed too, from one thirty-six to one fifty-two. And every rancher who has to buy feed this winter pays that difference. Follow The Cattle Ledger.
