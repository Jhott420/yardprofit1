## Script
> Calf prices are down about a hundred twenty-five dollars a head from a year ago. So are cattle prices going up or down? USDA's early October estimate for five-hundred-pound steer calves at Oklahoma City is about four thirty-two per hundred pounds. Last October it was four fifty-six. That's down about five percent. Heavier seven-hundred-seventy-five-pound feeders fell about six and a half percent. Finished steers slipped only about two percent. And prices peaked this June, at about four seventy-eight, so most of the drop came in just four months. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (USDA ERS Livestock prices, all in facts/beef.md):
- OKC 500–550 lb: 456.45 (Oct 2025) → 432.25 (Oct 2026 est.) = −5.3%; −$24.20/cwt × 5.25 ≈ −$127/head
- OKC 750–800 lb: 376.02 → 351.75 = −6.5%
- Nebraska fed steers: 228.73 → 223.50 = −2.3%
- June 2026 peak: 477.95

The whole year-over-year drop happened after the June peak, so "most of the drop came in 4 months" is, if anything, an understatement. No cause is stated, because none is sourced. The description notes the October figures are an early estimate and that this isn't a forecast.
