## Script
> A five-hundred-twenty-five-pound calf sold for about two thousand seventy dollars in September. That's USDA's price at the Oklahoma City sale, about three ninety-four per hundred pounds. So how much does a cow cost? It depends on how big it is. A seven-hundred-seventy-five-pound feeder steer brought about twenty-seven hundred dollars. A finished steer, at the average slaughter weight of about fourteen hundred fifty pounds, was worth about thirty-two hundred. The bigger the animal, the more it costs per head, but the less it costs per pound. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (all in facts/beef.md, USDA ERS, Sept 2026):
- 525 lb × $3.9401 = $2,069
- 775 lb × $3.4769 = $2,695
- 1,451 lb × $2.2167 = $3,216
