## 3. Editing instructions (iPhone, CapCut, free)
1. **New project.** Add your clips in the order above. Tap the canvas button and choose **9:16**.
2. **Voice.** Tap **Audio → Extract** (or import the narration MP3). If you don't have one, use **Text → Text-to-speech** and pick a deep narrator voice.
3. **Trim.** Trim each clip to the times in the table. Cut on the pauses in the voice. The "weight / breed / age / condition / market" clips should be quick, about one second each.
4. **Captions.** Tap **Text → Auto captions**. Use a bold white style with a black outline, placed in the middle of the screen. Add the big keyword (WEIGHT, BREED…) at the top in **yellow #FFD400** and **Montserrat Black** or a similar heavy font.
5. **Music.** Add a low, cinematic documentary track from CapCut's library at **10–15% volume**.
6. **Export.** 1080p, 30 fps. Upload from the YouTube app with **Create → Short**.
