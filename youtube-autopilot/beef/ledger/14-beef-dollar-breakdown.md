## Script
> A pound of Choice beef cost about nine ninety-four at the store in September. Here's where that money goes. About four ninety goes to the people who raised the animal: ranchers and feedlots. The packer keeps about seventy cents. And about four thirty-three covers everything after: cutting, shipping, and the grocery store. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (USDA ERS Meat Price Spreads, Choice beef, Sept 2026, already in facts/beef.md):
- Retail value: 994.4¢/lb
- Net farm value: 490.7¢ (49.3%)
- Wholesale 561.6¢, so the farm-to-wholesale (packer) spread is 70.9¢ (7.1%)
- Wholesale-to-retail spread: 432.8¢ (43.5%)
