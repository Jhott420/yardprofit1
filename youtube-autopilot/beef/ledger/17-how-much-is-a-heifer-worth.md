## Script
> A four-hundred-seventy-five-pound heifer calf was worth about eighteen hundred dollars in September. So how much is a heifer worth compared to a steer? At the Oklahoma City sale, USDA had four-hundred-fifty to five-hundred-pound heifers at about three eighty-one per hundred pounds. Steer calves, a little heavier, brought three ninety-four. At around seven hundred pounds, the gap gets wider: heifers about three thirty, steers about three forty-eight. Pound for pound, heifers sell for less, even when they're lighter. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used** (USDA ERS Livestock prices, OKC, Sept 2026, $/cwt; all in facts/beef.md):
- Heifers 450–500 lb: 381.35, so 475 lb × $3.8135 = $1,811
- Steers 500–550 lb: 394.01
- Heifers 700–750 lb: 329.59
- Steers 750–800 lb: 347.69
- Gap: 12.66 at the light end, 18.10 at the heavy end

No reason is given for the heifer discount, because none is sourced.
