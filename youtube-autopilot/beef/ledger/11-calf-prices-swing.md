# The Cattle Ledger — Short #11: "Calf Prices Just Dropped $300 a Head 📉"

**Length:** about 27 s · **Voice:** vidIQ "Brian" · Stock footage only

## Script
> A five-hundred-pound steer calf lost about three hundred dollars in value in just two months. In July, calves that size at Oklahoma City brought about four fifty-five per hundred pounds, USDA says. By September, three ninety-four. Then October's early estimate bounced back to four thirty-two. That's how fast a rancher's paycheck can swing. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used:** USDA ERS Livestock prices, Oklahoma City feeder steers M&L #1, 500–550 lb, $/cwt.
- Jul 2026: 454.77
- Sep 2026: 394.01, a drop of $60.76/cwt (about $304 on a 500-lb calf)
- Oct 2026 estimate: 432.25

The script deliberately gives no cause for the drop, because I have no sourced explanation.
