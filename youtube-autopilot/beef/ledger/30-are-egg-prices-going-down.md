## Script
> Farmers are getting about seventy-four percent less for their eggs than they did a year ago. So are egg prices going down? USDA says farm-level egg prices in October 2026 were seventy-four point four percent lower than in October 2025. At the store, USDA forecasts retail egg prices will fall about twenty-nine and a half percent this year. Why were eggs so expensive? Bird flu cut laying flocks, and retail eggs jumped about twenty-two percent in 2025. Now egg production is recovering. Subscribe to The Farm Ledger. Next: will beef prices keep going up?
