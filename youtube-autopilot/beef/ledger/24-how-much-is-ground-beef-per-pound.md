## Script
> Ground beef costs about six seventy-nine a pound right now. So how much is ground beef per pound? The Labor Department's price survey put it at six dollars and seventy-nine cents in September 2026. Ground chuck was about six forty-eight, up sixteen percent from five fifty-eight a year earlier. Compare that with about twelve sixty-nine for the average steak, and nine nineteen for a chuck roast. So even after the jump, ground beef still costs about half as much as steak. Subscribe. Next: how much is a feeder calf worth?
