## Script
> A pound of sirloin steak cost about fourteen dollars and eleven cents in September. So how much is steak per pound right now? That's the U.S. average for USDA Choice boneless sirloin, from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. All uncooked steaks together averaged about twelve sixty-nine a pound. Round steak was about nine eighty-four, and a chuck roast about nine nineteen. Ground beef, the cheapest, was six seventy-nine. Meanwhile, the steer itself sold for about two twenty-one a pound, live. Follow The Cattle Ledger.
