# The Cattle Ledger — Short #5: "How Much Does It Cost to Feed a Cow?"

**Length:** about 42 s · **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920 · **Voice:** documentary narrator (vidIQ "Brian")

## 1. Final script / voiceover text
> This is what a cow eats in a year, and what it costs. The USDA tracks feed costs for beef cows on cow-calf ranches.
> In 2025, it came to about four hundred thirty-six dollars per cow.
> About one hundred eighty-six of that is hay and feed the rancher grows and harvests. About one hundred fifty-six is the pasture the cow grazes. And about ninety-five is feed the rancher has to buy.
> That's roughly a dollar twenty a day, every single day, rain or drought.
> Feed is the biggest cash cost on the ranch: nearly two thirds of all operating costs.
> Follow The Cattle Ledger for the truth behind the beef business.

**Facts used:** USDA ERS Commodity Costs and Returns, cow-calf, U.S. total, 2025 ($ per cow). See `facts/beef.md`.
- Total feed: 436.35
- Homegrown harvested feed: 185.58
- Grazed feed: 155.92
- Purchased feed: 94.85
- Feed per day: 436.35 ÷ 365 = $1.20
- Feed as a share of operating costs: 436.35 ÷ 676.45 = 64.5%
