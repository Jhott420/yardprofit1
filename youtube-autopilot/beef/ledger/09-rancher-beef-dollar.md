# The Cattle Ledger — Short #9: "How Much of Your Beef Dollar Does the Rancher Get? 🤠"

**Length:** about 22 s · **Voice:** vidIQ "Brian"

## Script
> Out of every dollar you spend on beef, the rancher gets about forty-nine cents. That's USDA's number for Choice beef this September. The meat packer keeps about seven cents. And about forty-four cents goes to cutting, shipping, and the store. A year ago, ranchers got about fifty-five cents. Follow The Cattle Ledger.

**Facts used:** USDA ERS Meat Price Spreads, shares of the Choice beef retail dollar.
